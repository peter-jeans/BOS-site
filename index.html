How BOS works in Codex and Claude Code

BOS works as one helper: Local BOS keeps this app’s plan and decisions in its folder on your computer, while Cloud BOS provides online guidance and checks through your My BOS account. In Claude Code or Codex, just type “Ask BOS to…” or “Use BOS to…”; BOS explains any connection or approval needed.

Set up your tool once. Connect each project.

  1. Install BOS in your coding tool or workspace. Sign in to My BOS and choose your guide. It has the screenshots and any available downloads.
  2. Connect the app or project you want BOS to help with. Open it in your coding tool, review its setup, and verify it in My BOS. Repeat this for each project.
