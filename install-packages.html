What the dates and evidence mean

This page reads a published catalogue. Refreshing it checks for a newer publication; it does not run tests, connect to your account or inspect your app.

Package maturity is retained in each item’s evidence detail. It measures recorded packaging and readiness evidence, not how healthy your app is or a percentage completed. Older evidence needs review before relying on it.

If a refresh fails, the last displayed catalogue remains visible with a warning. No account or private project information is requested here.
