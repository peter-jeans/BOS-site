How these indicators stay current

This page checks the latest published catalogue when opened, every minute while visible, and when you press Refresh status. The catalogue is generated from BOS package, entitlement and maturity records during publication. Evidence can be older than the publication date; these indicators are not a live connection to your app and do not measure its health.

If a refresh fails, the last displayed catalogue remains visible with a warning. No account, project, installation history or private app data is included.
