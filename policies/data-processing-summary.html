Data Processing Summary

Supplier: Peter Jeans · ABN 84 050 238 060 · Australia.

What stays in your project and what reaches Cloud BOS

Project source and detailed memory stay in your project-controlled environment by default. Cloud BOS handles account, connection, access and compact service metadata. Your AI/development platform processes information under its own arrangements. This summary is not a separate negotiated data-processing agreement.

Reading guide: this summary explains the full policy below. Mandatory rights and the full policy prevail.

Local projects and development hosts

Local BOS project files can contain context, instructions, decisions, build intentions and receipts. Base44 or another cloud development platform holds its project files within that provider's environment rather than on your computer. Your chosen AI host may read and process files or prompts using its own providers. Keep existing permissions, licence conditions and privacy obligations in mind when choosing those tools.

Cloud BOS

Cloud BOS uses Cloudflare-hosted service/database infrastructure and WorkOS authentication. It handles account/contact and identity state, project bindings, scoped connection and access records, exact agreement references and submitted acknowledgements, compact service outcomes and permitted sanitised learning categories. Essential authentication and technical provider metadata may also be processed. See the Privacy Policy for purposes, providers, overseas processing, retention and requests.

Excluded from default central collection

Raw source, app databases, private customer records, secrets, screenshots, raw logs, full prompts, complete conversations and whole project histories are not part of default Cloud BOS collection or the central agreement/defence archive. Do not upload them through unsupported flows. Minimise and redact necessary support correspondence.

Learning and future transfers

The required resolved-pattern contribution is limited to sanitised categories and verified outcomes. It is distinct from identifiable case studies or richer project transfer. A future cloud capsule, evidence bundle or broader sync feature needs a separately supported flow with clear scope, purposes, information categories and choices. Its mention here does not make it available or authorise a transfer.

Requests and project records

Access, correction, account closure and deletion requests are handled through peterjeans2@gmail.com with appropriate identity verification and lawful retention exceptions. Removing an account does not remove copies held in your own project or by independent providers. Automated universal export/deletion and a negotiated enterprise processor agreement are not included in the current beta.
