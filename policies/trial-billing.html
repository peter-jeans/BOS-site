Trial and Billing

Last updated: 16 September 2026.

Free beta, then a gradual handover

Invited beta access is free within verified capabilities. After beta, the approved direction is a generous starter allowance, limited ongoing help and retained project continuity, with optional non-renewing Build Passes or recurring membership. Standard prices are approved; allowances and retention terms are being scoped. Checkout is not live.

This is a draft description, not an active paid offer or an account entitlement.

Read the full draft

Current invited beta

No BOS access charge applies during the invited beta. An invitation and verified access are still required. The earlier Standard/Plus/Pro beta sales prices are superseded as public offers; existing account commitments are not silently changed.

Third-party development-platform subscriptions, hosting, model usage and credits are separate. Any applicable taxes and payment charges must be disclosed before a future purchase.

Planned post-beta access

The starter allowance should support repeated useful work across sessions. Show limits and options from the beginning, with advance notice and a wrap-up reserve inside the allowance for an already-started bounded workflow. Do not interrupt that workflow for payment or promise unlimited project completion.

After the introduction, retain readable/exportable project records under published retention terms and a small defined allowance of new help. Users may choose a one-off Build Pass with no automatic renewal or an optional recurring membership with explicit consent and cancellation terms.

These stages and their account meters are not active yet. Standard prices and the seven-day pass duration are approved as described below. Allowances, beta end date, refunds, taxes, existing-account transition and retention still need final terms before launch. No automatic charge or paid conversion is authorised by this draft.

BOS stabilisation stays with your app.

Your installed build rules, existing checks, decisions and project memory remain in place. A pass or subscription funds additional active governance—new reviews, scans, planning and guidance.

Existing checks depend on your development tools. Cloud-powered help uses your service allowance; platform, hosting and AI costs remain separate.

Support and evidence of value

Included support is self-service guidance and a structured channel for plugin-defect reports. Open-ended personal support and guaranteed response times are not included. Separately purchased human assistance would require its own scope and terms.

Distinguish verified contributions from estimates. Do not count repeated warnings, failed attempts or BOS-defect repair as new completed value. Savings estimates and inferred trust scores do not determine billing.

Billing implementation

  • No post-beta price is approved for sale; invited beta access is requested and approved separately.
  • Payment processing should be handled by a payment provider such as Stripe.
  • Cloud BOS should decide entitlement from verified backend events, not from browser state or client claims.
  • Base44 apps should never receive payment-provider secrets or Cloudflare admin tokens.

Plan limits

Capabilities may be limited by plan, app count, scan count, support level, rate limits, advisory catalogue access, or beta availability.

Approved Standard prices after beta

Beta users receive free BOS access until beta ends, within their enabled capabilities. You will receive advance notice and will not be charged automatically.

A Standard pass will cost A$15 for seven days from activation, without renewal. Standard subscription access will cost A$29/month; cancellation stops future renewal. Sales are not open. Usage allowances, activation details and complete billing terms will be published before purchase.
