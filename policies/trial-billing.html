Starting and sharing the trial

After verified sign-in and acceptance of the current policies, the first verified project connection starts one calendar month of free active BOS help. All your account's projects share the displayed expiry. Signup alone does not start the trial. Another project, reinstallation, a new tool or accepting updated terms does not restart it. My BOS shows the applicable account and project-access state.

Trial and complimentary access

The active trial includes the highest customer product tier without commercial feature, project-count, scan-count or workflow-call quotas. An expressly administered account-wide complimentary grant provides the same access to verified current and future projects owned by that account until its expiry or revocation. A grant without an expiry continues until revoked. An existing project-specific exception keeps its original scope and is not automatically widened.

Identity, ownership, project scope, security/abuse protections, supported platform capabilities and access dates still apply. Customer product access does not confer founder/operator authority or include unlimited human assistance. Your development platform, AI/model, hosting and other provider charges remain separate.

After trial expiry or grant revocation

After trial expiry, already-started trial workflows may finish within a seven-day window; this does not start new workflows or promise project completion. Fresh cloud guidance needs another valid access entitlement. Installed project rules, existing checks and saved project-controlled decisions/memory remain, subject to their tool dependencies. BOS does not erase those files merely because the trial ends.

A complimentary grant ends at expiry or revocation. Other valid trial, paid or separately scoped access remains; ending a grant does not restart a trial or cancel a subscription. There is no promise of perpetual cloud access. Retention of account/service records is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Current billing position

Paid checkout is not live. We do not require a card for the free month and will not automatically charge or convert it into a paid subscription. Stripe/payment integrations or a displayed future price do not themselves authorise a purchase. Cloud BOS owns effective access state; a payment-provider redirect is not proof of entitlement.

Future paid offers

The planned Standard Build Pass is A$15 for seven days from activation, without automatic renewal; the planned Standard subscription is A$29 per month. These are future offers, not currently purchasable services. Before any sale, the checkout must disclose the total payable price including applicable taxes and unavoidable charges, scope/allowances, activation, renewal/cancellation and refund terms. Any change to those planned offers must be disclosed before purchase. You must expressly agree to any recurring charge.

Support and cancellations

Current included support is self-service guidance and plugin-defect reporting. No guaranteed response time or open-ended personal development service is included. Any purchased human assistance would need a separate scope and agreement. You can stop using the free service at any time; see Refund and Cancellation for account closure and future paid-service protections.
