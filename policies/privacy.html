Operator and scope

Peter Jeans, ABN 84 050 238 060, Australia, operates BOS and Cloud BOS. Contact peterjeans2@gmail.com. This notice covers our account, hosted connection, operational and support information. Your AI/development host and other services have separate information-handling practices.

Information we handle

  • Verified email/contact information, account/user references, identity-provider references or hashes, account membership and sign-in/session status.
  • Project and platform references, connection and installation state, accepted project-plan references, access grants and trial dates.
  • Agreement versions, accepting actor and time, submitted acknowledgements and necessary access/security or dispute evidence.
  • Compact request, capability, version, outcome, error-category and access/security metadata; credential hashes/lifecycle metadata and permitted sanitised resolved-pattern categories.
  • Information you choose to send for support. Hosting and identity providers also handle technical request, browser/network and sign-in information. Essential session and sign-in cookies are used; blocking them may prevent sign-in.

Small or hashed records can still be personal information. We do not represent all BOS records as anonymous. We collect what you provide, what your authorised tools send through supported flows, verified identity results and service activity needed to operate the connection.

Purposes and choices

We use information to authenticate users, connect authorised projects, provide requested guidance, manage access and agreements, protect the service, diagnose defects, handle support and legal claims, and improve BOS through the agreed sanitised learning contribution. We do not sell personal information or use project histories for advertising profiles. Without necessary account and connection information, we cannot provide the relevant hosted service.

The required learning acknowledgement covers category-level lessons from verified BOS-guided work, not raw code, app data, personal records, secrets or full prompts. Optional attribution, case studies and richer project transfers require a separate supported choice. Stop Cloud BOS use and contact us if you wish to stop future required contributions or close your account. Existing lawful records and non-identifying lessons may remain as explained below.

Project material and AI tools

Detailed project memory and source remain in your project-controlled environment by default. Do not send secrets, private customer data, database exports, full chats or raw logs to Cloud BOS through an unsupported flow. Limit and redact support submissions. If you send material accidentally, tell us so that we can assess appropriate containment or removal.

The AI coding host you choose may read or process your project under its own permissions and terms, including through its model providers. This notice does not change those arrangements. The current Cloud BOS service is a guidance/metadata service; this notice does not authorise general cloud upload of your source or whole project memory.

See the provider notices for Cloudflare, WorkOS, GitHub and Google.

Information may also be disclosed to authorised support or professional advisers where necessary, or where required or permitted by applicable law. We do not routinely send entire project histories to advisers or insurers.

Retention and security

We retain information for active service needs and, where reasonably necessary afterwards, security, agreement/access evidence, resolving complaints or claims and legal obligations. Retention depends on the record and purpose; it is not a promise to keep every record forever. Exact public policy text is archived once per version; acceptance records refer to it. We do not create a central archive of whole customer projects to defend possible claims.

Expiry of cloud access does not erase your local project records. Account closure does not necessarily delete agreement evidence or a narrowly scoped legal hold immediately. Requests are reviewed manually; automated account-wide export, scheduled disposal and instant provider-wide deletion are not currently offered. Provider backups and records may have separate lawful retention. We will explain relevant retention grounds or limits when handling a request.

We use access restrictions, scoped connections and credential/session protections appropriate to the supported service. No system is completely secure. We will handle security incidents and required notifications under the laws that apply.

Access, correction, deletion and complaints

Email peterjeans2@gmail.com with your request and enough information to locate the account, without sending passwords or tokens. We may need to verify your identity. We will consider applicable access, correction, deletion, objection and other rights, and explain any lawful exception or practical limitation. We aim to respond to privacy complaints within 30 days, or sooner if required by law; if more time is needed, we will explain why.

You may complain to the relevant privacy regulator where it has jurisdiction, including the OAIC in Australia where applicable. This policy does not assert that every privacy law applies to every customer or remove any mandatory privacy right. Material changes will be notified through the account or contact channel; any required fresh consent will be obtained.
