Permitted use

You may use BOS to plan, build, review and maintain legitimate software projects you are authorised to access. Follow the applicable platform rules, licences, account permissions and project scope. Keep your access credentials secure.

Prohibited conduct

  • Malware, credential theft, harmful exploitation, unauthorised surveillance, unlawful scraping or other unlawful or abusive activity.
  • Bypassing identity, ownership, subscription, access or security protections, or interfering with the service or another user.
  • Submitting secrets, passwords, private keys, payment details, regulated customer records or third-party confidential material through a Cloud BOS flow that does not expressly support it.
  • Attempting to obtain private BOS source, internal material or another user's data without permission. Rights expressly granted by an applicable open-source licence remain unaffected.

Restrictions and review

We may reject requests or restrict affected access where reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, suspected abuse or a material breach. A security safeguard is not a commercial usage quota. Where lawful and practicable, we will explain the reason and give a reasonable opportunity to correct a breach or request review at peterjeans2@gmail.com. Urgent protection may precede notice. The Terms of Use govern suspension, ending service and mandatory rights.
