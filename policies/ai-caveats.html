Understand what a check result means

BOS and AI can be wrong. A receipt shows attempted work and available checks, not certification. Review proposed changes, verify a recovery path and run suitable tests before release. Instructions and approval prompts do not guarantee correct behaviour.

Reading guide: this summary explains the full policy below. Mandatory rights and the full policy prevail.

Read the full policy

Guidance and execution

BOS can structure, route and check AI-assisted development, but its guidance can be incomplete, stale, overbroad or wrong. Your AI/development host may execute file changes, commands or deployment operations using permissions you grant. Read the target, scope and proposed action before approval. Generated advice and an executed tool action are different things; both can fail.

No promised result

BOS does not guarantee effectiveness, faster development, savings, completion, business success or detection/prevention of every defect, security issue or loss. Model changes, context, integrations and how the product is used affect results. A successful test, benchmark or another user's experience does not guarantee your outcome.

Evidence and safeguards

Receipts report attempted actions, available evidence and checks actually performed. They do not prove complete correctness, independent certification or compliance. A displayed role, header, installed control file or connected plugin does not establish that its instructions were followed. A backup acknowledgement does not prove a usable backup exists.

Your review

  • Review changed source and relevant runtime behaviour before release.
  • Use appropriate tests and manual checks, and verify recoverable backups for material changes.
  • Protect credentials, production data and permissions; use qualified advice where the project needs it.
  • Treat warnings and blocks as operational signals, not a guarantee that all other actions are safe.
